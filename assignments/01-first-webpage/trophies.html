The Trophy Room

A closer look at FC Barcelona's greatest achievements over the years.

Domestic Trophies

La Liga: 29 titles.

Copa del Rey: 32 titles: the most of any club in Spanish football.

Supercopa de España: 16 titles: The most of any club in Spanish football.

European Trophies

UEFA Champions League: 5 titles (1992, 2006, 2009, 2011, 2015).

UEFA Super Cup: 5 titles.

Ballon d'Or Winners

FC Barcelona has produced more Ballon d'Or winners than any other club in the world. Here are the men who won it while playing for the Club:

Luis Suárez (1960)

Johan Cruyff (1973, 1974)

Hristo Stoichkov (1994)

Rivaldo (1999)

Ronaldinho (2005)

Lionel Messi (2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019)

On the women's side, Alexia Putellas (2021, 2022) and Aitana Bonmatí (2023, 2024, 2025) have also brought home the award.
